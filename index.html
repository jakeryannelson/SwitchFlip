Four things we do.

Most people start with the first or second. Each one makes the next easier, but you don't have to do all four.

1A new website

If yours is old, slow, or you're a little embarrassed to send people to it, we rebuild it. You get a site that works on a phone and that you can edit yourself. If you don't have email on your own domain or a link people can use to book time with you, we set those up too.

2Claude in the apps you already use

We set Claude up for your team and connect it to Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Slack, your CRM, your accounting software, whatever you run on. After that it can read the email, check the calendar, find the file, and draft the reply. We train your people on how to use it and write down the rules so nobody has to guess. This usually takes days, not months.

3An agent that does a job

Once Claude is connected, we can give it a job to own. Chasing overdue invoices. Sorting the shared inbox. Scheduling. Intake forms. The Monday report. It runs on a schedule, follows the rules you set, keeps a log of what it did, and hands anything unusual to a person.

4Bigger builds

For companies that outgrow the first three. Custom systems, several agents working together, deeper connections into the software the business runs on. Scoped one project at a time, built with your people watching.

How it goes

Same shape whatever we're doing. We don't push you toward a direction. We work on what you actually want fixed, then throw out a couple of ideas you haven't thought of. In that order.

Week 0A call

Thirty minutes. You tell us what's slow. We tell you what's realistic and what isn't. Sometimes the answer is "don't use AI for that."

Week 1One thing, scoped

One site, one connection, or one job, with a clear before and after, a fixed price, and a fixed date. We measure the before so the after means something.

Weeks 2 to 5Built with you watching

Inside your systems, with your people in the loop. You see it running on real work before you pay the balance.

Week 6Handed over

Docs, training, a runbook, and the keys. It's yours. If you want us around after that, we'll talk about it then.

Is this a fit?

We'd rather tell you now than waste your Tuesday.

Probably, if you're

  • A business of any size whose back office runs on email, PDFs, and spreadsheets. Two-person shop or five hundred people, same mess
  • The person who owns operations, finance, or customer support, or the founder still doing all three
  • Somewhere between "we should really update the website" and "we need an agent that chases invoices," and not sure which one to do first
  • Tired of being told "AI will fix that" by people who can't show you one thing it fixed

Probably not a fit if

  • You want a strategy deck or a report about AI. We build working things.
  • You want it all done at once. We start with one job, get it working, then do the next.
  • You won't connect Claude to the tools where the work happens, like your email, files, or accounting software.
  • Nobody on your side can spend an hour a week with us. We need the person who does the job today.
  • You want AI making final calls on money, hiring, or legal with no person signing off.
