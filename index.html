A few places most businesses start.

Each one makes the next easier, and you don't have to do all four.

1 A new website

If your website is out of date or hard to use on a phone, we rebuild it so it works well on any screen and you can update it yourself. We can also set up email on your own domain and a link customers can use to book time with you.

2 Claude in the apps you already use

We set up Claude for your team and connect it to the software you already use, such as your email, calendar, files, and accounting. Once it's connected, Claude can look things up across those apps and draft replies and documents for you. We train your team to use it and write down clear rules for what it should and shouldn't do. Setup usually takes a few days.

3 An agent that speeds up routine work

Once Claude is connected, an agent can make routine jobs go much faster. It does the first pass on work such as following up on overdue invoices, sorting a shared inbox, or scheduling appointments, and your team stays in charge of the result. The agent follows rules you set and keeps a record of everything it does. When something unusual comes up, it passes it to a person.

4 Bigger builds

Larger companies sometimes need more than the first three. We build custom systems where several agents work together and connect more deeply to the software your business runs on. We plan and price each project separately and build it with your team involved.

How it goes.

Every project follows the same four steps. We start with the problem you want fixed, and we suggest other ideas only after that's handled.

Week 0

A call

We talk for thirty minutes about what's slowing you down. We'll tell you plainly whether AI is a good fit for it, and sometimes it isn't.

Week 1

One project, agreed

We agree on one project with a fixed price and a finish date. We also measure how the work gets done today so we can show what changed.

Weeks 2 to 5

Built with your team

We build it inside your own systems and keep your team involved. You see it working on real tasks before you pay the balance.

Week 6

Handed over

We train your team and give you written instructions and full access. You own it from then on. Our goal is for you to stop needing us, and ongoing support is there if you want it.

Is this a fit?

Here is who we tend to help the most, and who we don't.

Probably a fit if

  • Your office work runs mostly on email, PDFs, and spreadsheets.
  • You run operations, finance, or customer support, or you're an owner doing all of them.
  • You know some of this would help but aren't sure where to start.
  • You've heard a lot of AI promises and want to see something that works.

Probably not a fit if

  • You're looking for a strategy report about AI. Our work is building things and setting them up.
  • You want everything done at once. We start with one job and move to the next after it's working.
  • You don't want Claude connected to the software where the work happens, such as your email, files, or accounting.
  • Nobody on your team can spend an hour a week with us. We need time with the person who does the job today.
  • You want AI to make final decisions on money, hiring, or legal matters without a person approving them.
